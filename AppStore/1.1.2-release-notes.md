# 长卷 1.1.2（Build 42）发布文案

## 此版本的新增内容

- 新增横向长截图，可左右往返查看表格后，按内容顺序拼成长图。
- 新增设置入口，可选择拼接方向与是否保留顶部状态栏。默认仍为纵向、去状态栏。
- 成功保存到照片后，自动释放原始画面与分析缓存。
- 为本地预览设置保留期限和空间上限，减少长期使用的存储积累；升级后也会检查旧版已保存记录。
- 新增「清理所有缓存」，明确确认后可释放所有原始画面与分析缓存，不删除系统照片或已生成的预览。
- 优化宽图预览，保持原有中英文界面、双向和跨页录制体验。
- 修复横向拼接在飞书表格等重复列内容中可能发生的错列、缺失与重叠；增强较大步幅和中途回滑时的位移判定。
- 改善控制中心重复启动共享面板时的恢复处理，保留显式重试入口并传递系统选择错误。
- 修复系统共享面板与灵动岛收起过程被误判为正文时，横向整理可能失败的问题。
- 修复横向表格截图中固定悬浮按钮重复出现的问题，识别后将整组控件保留一次，并从实际录制画面恢复可见正文。

## 审核备注补充（英文）

Version 1.1.2 adds a Settings sheet, accessible from the gear button on the home screen. It provides an explicit Vertical/Horizontal scroll direction, an optional captured status bar, and manual raw-frame/analysis-cache cleanup. Defaults preserve the existing vertical, cropped-status-bar experience. Options are frozen per recording; one recording uses one axis, including its manually separated pages. Horizontal results keep the captured status bar once when enabled. No system sharing consent is bypassed.

After a successful Photos transaction, the app automatically removes completed recordings' raw frames and analysis. Saved local previews are maintained under a 7-day, 10-session and 100 MB budget; active previews and notification delivery are protected. Unsaved/failed inputs are excluded from automatic pruning. Manual cleanup explicitly warns that unsaved input recovery is lost, requires confirmation, and is disabled during recording/processing. It retains Photos assets and generated previews. No photo-library read permission, backend or upload is added.

Actual device acceptance, upload, review submission and public availability are recorded separately in the release receipt; this copy alone does not prove acceptance or publication.
